# ThinkOrQuit: Academic Poster Design Note & Verification Record

## 1. Design Overview & Purpose

This design note documents the visual architecture, typography, color theory, and empirical source provenance for the academic research poster representing:

**"ThinkOrQuit: Sequential Conformal Optimal Stopping and Selective Abstention for Reliable Large Language Models"**

The poster is designed for presentation at top-tier machine learning and artificial intelligence conferences (e.g., NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, COLM). It provides a high-density, mathematically rigorous, and visually restrained representation of test-time compute allocation and selective prediction under distribution shift.

## 2. Visual Architecture & Layout Specifications

- **Format & Dimensions**: 
  - Widescreen 16:9 Landscape Layout.
  - PowerPoint (`.pptx`): $48.0 \times 27.0$ inches ($121.92 \times 68.58\text{ cm}$).
  - Print-Ready Vector PDF: $3840 \times 2160\text{ px}$ (scales losslessly to A0 / Arch E landscape).
  - High-Resolution Raster Preview: $8000 \times 4500\text{ px}$ (300+ DPI equivalent, 8K ultra-sharp rendering).
- **Column Structure**: 
  - 4-column balanced grid with $0.6$-inch inter-column gutters and $0.8$-inch exterior margins.
  - Column 1: Research Motivation, The Overthinking & Overconfidence Pathologies, Literature Gap, Problem Formulation (SC-OSSA).
  - Column 2: Implemented Technical Architecture (Semantic Entropy, Martingale Drift, Covariate Shift Conformal Calibration), Benchmark Corpora, Zero-Leakage Data Governance.
  - Column 3: In-Distribution Benchmark Table (GSM8K Test Split, $N=132$), Risk-Coverage Tradeoff Curve (RC-AUC), Cross-Domain Distribution Shift Bar Chart.
  - Column 4: Audited Pre-registered Hypotheses (FWER $\alpha=0.05$ with Holm-Bonferroni correction), Demonstrated Contributions, Technical Limitations, Verified References (2024–2026).

## 3. Scientific Palette & Typography

### Palette Rationale
- **Primary Header & Accents**: Deep Academic Navy (`#0F2042` / `RGB(15, 32, 66)`) conveys institutional authority and technical precision.
- **Secondary Metric & Focus Highlight**: Calibrated Teal (`#0D9488` / `RGB(13, 148, 136)`) draws eye path to key findings, proposed method metrics, and supported hypotheses.
- **Error & Failure Contrast**: Muted Crimson (`#DC2626` / `RGB(220, 38, 38)`) highlights baseline failure modes, overconfidence risks, and rejected hypothesis bounds without sensory fatigue.
- **Card Surfaces & Grid**: Clean White (`#FFFFFF`) with Slate-50 background (`#F8FAFC`) and Slate-200 structural borders (`#E2E8F0`) ensures maximum contrast in ambient conference hall lighting.

### Typography Hierarchy
- Poster Title: 52pt / 36pt Bold (Sans-Serif, High Legibility).
- Subtitle: 28pt / 20pt Semi-Bold.
- Section Headers: 24pt / 15pt Bold Uppercase with Teal 4px accent indicator.
- Section Body: 18.5pt / 13pt Regular with $1.45$ line height.
- Mathematical Formulas & Metrics: 17.5pt / 12pt Monospace / Bold.
- Captions & Citations: 15.5pt / 10pt Regular Slate.
